Level 1

🧭 When do we use the Present Simple?

Use it for things that are true in general — not just "now".

🔁 Habits & routines I brush my teeth every morning.
She goes to school by bus.
🌍 Facts & truths Water boils at 100°C.
Cats like fish.
🕐 Timetables & schedules The train leaves at 8:00.
The shop opens at 9.

⏰ Signal words (they love the Present Simple!)

always usually often sometimes never every day / week / year on Mondays once a week in the morning

Level 2

➕ How to form a positive sentence

I / You / We / They + verb (no change)
He / She / It + verb + s / es 👈 don't forget the -s!
SubjectVerbExample
I / You / We / TheyplayThey play football on Saturdays.
He / She / ItplaysShe plays the guitar.
He / She / ItwatchesHe watches TV after dinner.
He / She / ItstudiesMy sister studies English.

🔐 Secret code: how to add -s / -es

RuleVerb → he/she/it
Most verbs: + swork → works • live → lives • like → likes
Ends in -ch, -sh, -ss, -s, -x, -o: + es watch → watches • finish → finishes • go → goes • do → does
Consonant + y: y → ies study → studies • carry → carries
Vowel + y: just + s play → plays • buy → buys
⭐ Irregular bosshave → has
⚠️ Careful! The verb be is special and does NOT follow these rules:
I am • you/we/they are • he/she/it is. It also doesn't use do/does.

Level 3

➖ How to form a negative sentence

I / You / We / They + don't (do not) + verb
He / She / It + doesn't (does not) + verb
I don't like coffee. ☕🚫
They don't live in London.
She doesn't eat meat. 🥗
My brother doesn't watch TV.
⚠️ Trap alert! When you use doesn't, the -s goes away (it "jumps" onto does).
❌ She doesn't plays tennis.  →  ✅ She doesn't play tennis.
❌ He don't works here.  →  ✅ He doesn't work here.
💡 Short forms: do not = don't  •  does not = doesn't

Level 4

❓ How to form a yes/no question

Do + I / you / we / they + verb + ?
Does + he / she / it + verb + ?
🎤 Do you like ice cream? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
🎤 Do they play tennis? — Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
🎤 Does she speak French? — Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
🎤 Does it rain a lot here? — Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.

🎁 Short answers (never use the main verb here!)

Question✅ Yes…❌ No…
Do you…?Yes, I do.No, I don't.
Do they…?Yes, they do.No, they don't.
Does he…?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.
Does she…?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
⚠️ Trap alert! No -s on the verb in questions — the does already has it.
❌ Does she likes pizza?  →  ✅ Does she like pizza?

Level 5

🕵️ How to form a wh- question

Wh- word + do / does + subject + verb + ?

What Where When Why Who Which Whose How How often What time

Wh- wordQuestionAnswer
WhatWhat do you eat for breakfast?I eat cereal.

Level 6

🦸 How to form a "who" question

When "who" is the person who does the action, use this pattern.

"Who" = the SUBJECT (the doer) 🦸‍♂️

Who + verb (+ s / es) + … ?   NO do / does!
Who plays the piano? — Maria does. / Maria.
Who wants ice cream? — I do!
Who lives in this house? — My grandparents do.
💡 Why no "does"? Because who IS the subject — there is nobody else for does to help. The verb behaves like he/she/it, so it takes -s.
⚠️ Trap alert! Don't add do / does when “who” is the subject.
❌ Who does play the piano?  →  ✅ Who plays the piano?
❌ Who does live here?  →  ✅ Who lives here?

3. Who / help / you
